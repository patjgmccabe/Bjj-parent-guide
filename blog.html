Kids’ Divisions Take Center Stage at Day 2 of the UAE Jiu-Jitsu Championship

Day 2 of Round 7 of the Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Jiu-Jitsu Championship spotlighted the kids’ categories, with families filling the stands to support young athletes. A federation official said early competition builds discipline, confidence, and respect — and one parent said jiu-jitsu made his three sons more disciplined and confident.
